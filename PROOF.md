- **BIMI** — `default._bimi` TXT published, logo served live at
  https://khesf.com/bimi.svg.

## v7 addendum — benchmark board and transport evidence (2026-11-17)

The standing 10-domain board (BENCHMARK.md, regenerated by
`python3 -m sprint.benchmark`) puts khesf.com at **6.0/6 — the only
full-marks domain on the board**, ahead of cloudflare.com (5.2),
microsoft.com (5.0), and google.com (4.8). Scores are risk-weighted
(v4: DMARC 2.0, DKIM 1.5, SPF 1.0, MTA-STS/TLS-RPT/BIMI 0.5).
DKIM probing is dictionary-based (42 provider selectors); a domain
signing under an unlisted selector reads as warn, not fail.

Transport defects found and fixed in v4, recorded as
docs/adr/0001-dns-transport.md: DKIM keys hidden by 512-byte UDP
truncation (EDNS + TCP retry), resolver REFUSED under load read as
"no record" (rcode honesty + backoff), and mismatched datagrams
under parallel load (transaction-id + question-name verification).
The v4 board was run three times with identical results before
the version was accepted.

board, and a sample HTML deliverable).
